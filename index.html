SEC-01

The mission

Dissertation · Mechanical Engineering · FIU

Critical Infrastructure Assessment using Autonomous Swarm of Drones with Sensor Payloads

Aging nuclear and civil concrete structures need inspection in places that are dark, cramped, radioactive, and out of GPS range. I build heterogeneous robot teams that go in together: drones for the walls and ceilings, legged and climbing robots for contact sensing, each carrying different sensors. They scan, share what they find, hand suspicious spots to a teammate with the right sensor to confirm, and fuse it all into one picture of the structure. Working under Dr. Leonel Lagos at FIU's Applied Research Center.

SEC-02

Payload bay

No single sensor sees everything. The fleet carries a different mix on every airframe, and the value comes from combining them.

Time of flight · 3D

LiDAR

Millions of points: shape, spalling, deformation.

B-scan · subsurface

GPR + Ultrasonic

Into the concrete: rebar, voids, delamination.

VNIR / SWIR · hundreds of bands

Hyperspectral

A full spectrum per pixel. Chemistry shows up as a signature.

Long-wave infrared

Thermal

Moisture ingress and delamination as heat anomalies.

Gamma + neutron

Radiation

Calibrated detectors map dose fields and hotspots.

Beacons · echoes

Acoustic

Sound beacons for ranging and coordination where radio struggles.

RGB + depth

Vision

Cameras and depth for cracks, docking and obstacle avoidance.

Contact + vacuum

Suction climbing

Bellows-cup feet hold contact sensors on walls and ceilings.

SEC-03

The brains

The sensors collect. These layers decide what it means and what to do next.

Perception

Deep learning

Detection and segmentation of cracks, spalling, voids and corrosion across every modality.

Data

Generative AI

Synthetic rare defects and digital-twin data so models learn what real inspections almost never see.

Mission

Agentic AI

LLM and VLM agents read sensor evidence, plan the next pass, and hand off to the right teammate, with a safety shield that can veto them.

Learning

Federated learning

Each robot learns from its own sensor and shares compact model updates instead of raw data.

Edge

Edge AI

Onboard inference on companion computers so robots keep working when the link drops.

Trust

Cyber security

Anomaly detection and trust anchors that catch spoofed telemetry and rogue agents.
